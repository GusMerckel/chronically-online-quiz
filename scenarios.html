Fan to Fanatic Scenarios

As a group, put all six cards in order from "totally normal" at the top to "something's wrong" at the bottom. Drag a card by its number, or use the arrows.

Totally normal
    Something's wrong

    Talk it over

    • Where did your group disagree the most? Which two cards were hardest to separate?
    • Find the point on your list where "fan" turns into "fanatic." What changed?
    • Which cards affect only you, and which ones affect the creator or other people?
    • Have you ever felt like a creator was your friend? What makes that feeling so strong online?
